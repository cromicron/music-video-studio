[Exact arithmetic and dated source checks](paid-budget-v2.json).

**Keep the complete owned-LTX production as the foundation. For an optional paid variant, first test LongCat Video Distilled on fal with one $1 credit purchase. If it does not earn a place, the bounded higher-resolution alternative is one $10 Runway Dev allocation for Gen-4 Turbo. Both purchases together are $11, or €13.4827 under the stated tax/FX assumptions, leaving €6.5173 beneath the hard cap.** Do not buy both automatically. The first film can finish entirely free; paying must improve the actual result or save meaningful production time at equal accepted quality.

Compare against the owned-LTX result in the actual bridge, including the first full-force plea that follows. Both candidates still require independently checked identity, facial motion, cadence and cuts. Neither has demonstrated superior acting on this project.

## Capability and quality determine whether paying helps

[LongCat's current endpoint](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/longcat-video/distilled/image-to-video/480p) advertises **$0.005 per generated second** and commercial use. Its [actual OpenAPI](https://fal.ai/api/openapi/queue/openapi.json?endpoint_id=fal-ai%2Flongcat-video%2Fdistilled%2Fimage-to-video%2F480p) permits **1–60 fps and 17–961 frames**; 168 frames at 24 fps is schema-valid for this proposed seven-second test. The default is 162 frames at 15 fps. Do not confuse a valid setting with a measured result: inspect actual decoded cadence, duration and charge before expanding the batch. It takes a first image and prompt, but the retrieved schema has no last image or supplied-song audio input. The 480p output is lower resolution than our 896 × 512 free source; it only earns adoption through actual acting or useful time savings at equal accepted quality. Enlarging it does not create detail.

[Runway's current API pricing](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing/) lists **5 credits per second** for Gen-4 Turbo. Its [OpenAPI](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/openapi.json) accepts integer durations **2–10 seconds**, including seven, with a first image only. There is no supplied-song audio field. The web app's separate 5/10-second options do not govern this API. Keep ordinary output settings; do not silently switch to a more expensive model. Its higher native resolution is useful, but no Gen-4 Turbo project-specific keeper rate is established. The old Short's Runway-hosted footage came from Kling O3 Pro, not Gen-4 Turbo.

Neither paid candidate is selected for the four sung/shouted pleas or playful summer mouthing. Those require the existing original-song-conditioned LTX path and their own performance review. V2-35 is a nonverbal reaction, followed by a cut to another view, so its exact final guide pose need not be prescribed. No frozen tail, slowed footage, replayed interval or silent still substitution may conceal a failed paid take. If a source requires frame-rate conversion, explicitly review the actual derivative and cadence before it enters the strict 24 fps edit. A paid comparison that fails is replaced by newly accepted free motion for the complete slot.

For ten films to each receive one paid keeper from 28 attempts, **10/28 = 35.7%** of requests must yield an appropriate keeper. That is a required fraction, not a measured probability. Comparable later films get their own story, reference frame and source song; they do not reuse this film's acting. When either paid stage supplies the chosen slot, each full picture still contains **272.25 seconds of free LTX + 5.541667 seconds of paid footage = 277.791667 seconds**. If neither earns adoption, all 277.791667 seconds remain required from the free route. This guarantees neither paid keepers nor finished-film quality before testing.
