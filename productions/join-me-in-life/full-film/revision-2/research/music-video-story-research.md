# Story research for a genuinely new Join Me in Life film

Researched 7 November 2026. This is a source-backed craft comparison and a recommendation, not approval of a treatment or evidence that new animation passes. The research reads official project pages and interviews with the people who made the films. It does not claim complete real-time viewing of all seven reference films or prove that our free model can reproduce their production quality.

## Seven useful examples and the actual method to borrow

| Work and evidence | What its creators actually did | Application to this film |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SIAMÉS — Mr. Fear** (2018), [Rudo project/credits](https://rudocompany.com/works/mr-fear/) and [interview with director Pablo Roldán](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/rudo-company-siames-animation-091118) | The team researched film references and the song's layers, defined a two-protagonist story, tested characters/designs, and only then animated. They explicitly aimed beyond visual sensation. | Define what each person wants and what changes before requesting attractive frames. Make her an actor with choices, not a passive illustration of his lyrics. |
| **spktra — Spirit Jumper** (2026), [creator interview](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/music-videos/spirit-jumper-music-video-spktra-258671.html) | Josh Fagin wrote the story, edited the complete animatic before animation, and wanted a narrative readable with the music off. He reports discarding an earlier animation pass while the direction was still changing. | Cut the entire 277.776-second song with boards first. A new reader must reconstruct the causal story without captions. Experiment cheaply with staging before spending GPU hours on every shot. |
| **Porter Robinson & Madeon — Shelter** (2016), [official film/story](https://www.sheltertheanimation.com/) and [A-1 director, designer and producer interview](https://www.gizmodo.jp/article/a1-pictures-shelter/) | The production developed the room and character in detail, negotiated a concrete storyline instead of settling for impact alone, and tested picture timing against the music. Its artists and musician even altered song length for that film. | Establish a coherent room/door/seat layout and the precise visual turn at each song section. We borrow the picture/music negotiation, **not** permission to change this user's locked master. Recut boards to fit the original song. A memory should reveal new information, rather than repeat a pretty scene. |
| **Khruangbin — May Ninth** (2024), [co-director Jenny Lucia Mascia's project account](https://www.theartofsnap.com/khruangbinmayninth) | The team built a story of interconnection, protecting a warm painterly style across 3D, drawn design and cel animation. Its credits separate story, direction, design, animation and color. | Carry one visual idea through transformed situations: a closed domestic space becomes a chosen hospital threshold, then a place where someone stays. Coherence is a planned story/design task, not an automatic consequence of using the same model. |
| **K/DA — FIRESTARTER** (2026), [5 November interview with Fortiche director Marietta Ren and supervisor Rémy Terreaux](https://80.lv/articles/take-a-look-behind-animation-in-k-da-s-firestarter-music-video) | They synchronized storyboard, performance, effects and lighting to the song; studied and annotated movement reference; and treated camera as part of choreography. A visual score persisted into compositing. | Make a cue sheet of action accents, gaze changes, cuts and light changes. For each difficult interaction, specify contact/weight transfer and study reference before generation. Camera movement needs a story or musical reason. Do not imitate dance-video speed for this distressed narrative. |
| **Just Desserts — Tender** (2026), [director James Siewert's production interview](https://directorsnotes.com/2026/04/07/james-siewert-tender/) | One evolving rotating construction generates new events. The director distinguishes hypnotic elaboration from narrative setup and catharsis; the eventual disruption attacks the established visual structure. | Repetition of an underlying *idea* can work only when its state changes. Our film needs dramatic consequences, so an unchanged cup/corridor/face recurring under choruses fails the brief. If a graphic border is introduced, its later break must mean a choice, not just a flourish. |
| **Andrew Scotchie — Just Dream** (2026), [27 October report including artist/director statements](https://www.awn.com/news/andrew-scotchie-drops-hand-made-animated-just-dream-music-video) | Scotchie supplied a focused timestamped script; Callum Scott-Dyson translated it into cut-out staging and built individual elements around recognizable recurring characters. | A timestamped script should tell the animator what happens and why, not merely list a mood and location. A few recurring elements can support many distinct actions without recycling footage. |

These are method references, not a claim that every example is a universal best video. The contemporary examples make the research current; the older ones remain relevant for a short, emotional, mostly wordless animated story. The pipelines often took months or years and paid teams. Borrowing their planning methods does not establish equal output quality or unlimited free generation.

## What the first film missed

The delivered film's own accounting separates 115.083 seconds of new LTX action, 5.042 seconds of supplied embrace, 32.667 seconds of drawing exposures, 9.708 seconds of first/end holds, and 115.292 seconds of still artwork with camera movement. Zero additional cash is not an editorial rationale for using the final two categories. The user's criticism is a story/acting requirement, even where a file and its individual movement intervals passed technical review.

The next film needs **a different situation at each chorus**. The song's changed final refrain must be earned by decisions and behavior before it arrives. A return to the same room is legitimate if somebody now acts differently there. A reused shot, new crop of an old frame, or idle gesture does not accomplish that change.

## A practical production method

1. Choose one complete treatment and write each sequence as objective → obstacle → action → changed situation. Keep physical, emotional and geographical continuity explicit.
2. Make new boards for all story beats. Use the established character identities and drawn treatment, with a controlled room/wardrobe map. A contact sheet is an efficient planning artifact; its tiny panels are not automatically production-quality animation guides.
3. Cut a full-song animatic using actual master audio. Review once with song, and separately without captions or explanation. Give the reviewer no synopsis beforehand; ask what they understood and where nothing changes.
4. Make short contrasting motion tests for the riskiest new actions: offering/accepting accompaniment, doorway hesitation/choice, and the summer exchange. Inspect all their used frames and the action at normal speed. Change staging/prompt/guide when a take fails; endless unchanged retries teach nothing.
5. Animate distinct actions with overlap handles, join them on intention or music, and keep the original song intact. Longer continuous takes need actual testing; do not stretch a short take with endpoint holds to hit the schedule.
6. Review the complete story again after replacement. Anatomy and frame hashes are necessary technical checks, but do not answer whether a viewer is compelled to keep watching.

## Outside review questions

- Without a synopsis, what does she choose, what does he initially try, and what changes in his approach?
- What is different after chorus one, chorus two and the final chorus?
- Does the summer scene reveal an aspect of their relationship needed by the present action?
- Can each significant cut be explained by an action, a reaction, a revelation or a musical change?
- Does the ending show support and ongoing care without claiming a cure, death, or romantic rescue?
- Flag every repeated source interval, endpoint extension, and long passage whose only motion is camera drift. Any intentional exception must earn its place editorially, not through budget arithmetic.
