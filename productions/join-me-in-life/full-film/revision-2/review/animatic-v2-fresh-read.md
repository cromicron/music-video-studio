# Independent visual reading of the V2 animatic

The images tell a coherent story of a severely withdrawn young woman accepting accompaniment and eventually entering a room to receive professional help. The man cannot restore her former happiness by talking, touching her shoulder, offering music, or bringing a drink. His consequential choice is to stay with her while she struggles to act. Her consequential choice is to stand and enter the consultation herself. The ending extends care to the person who has been supporting her, without showing that her difficulty has been resolved.
