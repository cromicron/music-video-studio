## What now works

The record gift prompts the sister's concern and call; the call causes his journey to her home. His arrival enables her first choice to accept accompaniment. A shared memory gives his attempted music offer a motive; her refusal changes his method from trying to recover a response to accepting her boundary. An invitation to care makes a new choice possible. He must remain available without substituting his action for hers. Her entry changes the immediate situation, while his continued waiting pays the promise without claiming a cure.

The choruses now have distinct consequences: leaving isolation, having a boundary respected, and accepting a next step into care. Her refusal is particularly valuable: she does not exist only to receive his rescue. The complete intro and outro have actual tasks and consequences, so their unusually long durations can be designed instead of padded.

## Four decisions the master board brief must lock

| Issue | Concrete staging requirement before drawing the board |
| --- | --- |
| **Music prop and contact** | Choose one explicit object, not “earbud/token.” A pair of white wired earbuds and one small player can link the summer exchange to his present offer. Establish the pocket/hand, who holds what, her legible refusal and his putting it away. The prop must not materialize after the memory or disappear at the next shot. Account for the arm/hand used in the single embrace; any later release must be an intentional event. |
| **Performance is not aggression** | Establish his rehearsal/performance space in the opening call scene, before the bridge needs it. Use a consistent visual transition for the subjective vocal lane. Do not stage the screams as him confronting her face-to-face, or imply he leaves the hospital to give a concert. The fourth plea's change is his relinquishing pressure, not the biggest raised arm. |
| **One threshold sequence** | Select either the director's interrupted rise followed by a later completed rise, or the researcher's walk and doorway pause. Do not combine them into repeated aborted approaches. The doorway version can strand her there for roughly 32 seconds of bridge and pleas: board progressive attention/action and changed spatial relationship through that duration, or delay the actual arrival. A montage of his fearful face cannot make the time automatically earned. |
| **One active ending** | Choose a concrete sequence of care continuing and ordinary support outside: pickup/carry/set-down/return for the drinks, or the sister's arrival and an action supporting him. The sister is already introduced, so her return can broaden care; bringing the record box into the hospital must remain plausible rather than a symbolic delivery mechanism. Show enough ongoing consultation to keep the empty chair/door from implying loss. Avoid adding a second invitation/reunion simply to fill the last bars. |

These are board-design choices, not reasons to postpone drawing for more research. Root may resolve them in the master brief and begin the rough board now. No full book/page apparatus is needed for the selected grounded treatment.
