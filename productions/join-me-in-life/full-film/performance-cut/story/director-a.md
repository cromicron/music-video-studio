# Director A — the room is finished; the relationship is not

Independent directing proposal for the new performance cut, 8 November 2026. This is a fresh treatment under the latest user instruction, not an adopted edit, a motion verdict or a claim that all described shots already exist. The original take49 recording remains intact (277.776 seconds;6,667 picture frames at24fps). Song-section times below are planning anchors from `revision-2/story/music-cues.json`, not newly verified syllable/beat timing.

## The decisive change

Use three immediately legible image domains: the blue present-day kitchen, **one** warm private summer recollection, and the band on its recognisable stage. Remove every imagined-future/golden-window/season-plane passage. The band supplies the force and movement that her sealed-off room cannot answer. The movie supplies the person the performance is trying to reach. This is one continuous night, not a rescue itinerary or an explanatory clinical story.

The governing action is **finishing versus leaving something unfinished**. She has made ordinary things frighteningly complete. He cannot find the person inside that completeness. At the end, ordinary objects can remain imperfect and the two people can remain present, without a promise that the crisis is solved. Keep the user's grief/beauty combination, but the beauty belongs to their connection and her remembered vitality; it must not make her decision look like transcendence.

## Confirmed direction and inference

User requirements: the red-haired woman's depression, suicidality and emotional unreachability are the film story; the full band must play; actual organising must establish the calm before the storm; only the stupid-summer-song memory is needed; his recognition at the rehearsed-final-part line must be a major dramatic event. He already fears her calm at the beginning. He is frightened and caring, not an angry interrogator or triumphant saviour.

Directing interpretation: show a **cluster** of purposeful ordering, residual depletion, loss of reciprocal attention and his fear. A tidy room alone must not become a visual diagnostic test. Apparent calm can be a concerning change in context, but not every calm or organised person is at risk. The character's particular intention is part of this fiction; it is not proof of an inevitable outcome. The clinical research agent's current NIMH/AAS/Samaritans findings support that distinction. No method, attempt, death, farewell instructions or romantic release imagery is required.

## Opening: show the work of putting the room in order

A new real action is needed. Begin already inside the shared kitchen, not with a scenic exterior or band glamour shot. In a restrained medium-wide, she finishes folding and smoothing a dishcloth beside the empty drainer; shelves/records and the table are already unnaturally orderly. Her task is precise, economical, over-complete. He is present at the table behind/in the other part of frame, watching her rather than looking pleased. Let the hand finish its practical task, then stay on the **absence of an ordinary response**: no shared glance, no conversational opening, no sense that she expects another thing tomorrow.

This can be an8–10second new shot. Keep one simple contact action rather than generating a whole house-cleaning montage. The cloth is not a symbolic suicide prop; it is a believable household object. No written farewell, packed departure bag, pills or ominous means. A brief keyboard insert can cover her move from counter to chair; the next kitchen shot finds her seated with the cups. That is a motivated time elision, not a disappearing/teleporting body.

Then use the already reviewed C01 cup alignment and C02 sealed-off settling selectively. The audience sees her make the space complete before verse1 explains it. Delay the first complete band-wide reveal until the approximately23s texture change, or its nearest reviewed musical phrase. The opening piano can feature the keyboardist earlier for a few seconds, but do not let stage spectacle replace the disturbing domestic observation.

Her apparent peace should be an external observation, not a luminous subjective sanctuary. Keep ordinary cool light, normal lens geometry and independent breathing. Do not use soft-focus halos, glamorous relief, serene ascent, a literal tunnel towards light or an angelic closed-eye portrait. Her mental tunnel is shown through attention that fails to follow him and composition that remains fixed when he tries to reach her.

## The bridge is recognition, not simply a pretty smile before screaming

The causal chain must read **he watches → an ordinary finalising action ends → she offers the socially correct surface without reciprocal life → he recognises what this means → the plea breaks out**. Give the bridge more screen time to faces and reaction, not more effects.

1. On the old-book / songs-as-art thought, use a restrained stage Yaro portrait, then the real room. Band performance is revealed as an appeal, not a celebration of tragedy.
2. On her calm/certainty, let her make one last small ordinary adjustment and release it. The **release and return of the hands** matter more than the object. Her depleted gaze stays internally fixed. The person he knows from the summer is physically there but is not answering.
3. At the rehearsed-final-part phrase, hold a close film Yaro reaction long enough to see the thought arrive: small inhale interrupted, brow lifts, eyes become afraid, attempted words lose their shape. Avoid an angry scowl. Keep his eye line towards her, not the camera. **Do not cut away on the recognition word to an instrument.** The audience must finish the inference with him.
4. First plea: a direct, full-force close delivery (the existing accepted C29 performance is a strong candidate). No preparatory weak first version, no dissolve. Let the cut and vocal attack break the prior composure.
5. Second plea: change angle/scale rather than recycle the first. One band impact insert can be considered only if it does not remove the complete desperate attack. Third plea may reach a wider stage/ensemble or isolated player on the gang-vocal expansion; fourth returns to the human voice/face and spent effort. The actual accepted C29–C32 films can remain as the vocal spine; use new band cutaways around them rather than regenerating good acting unnecessarily.
6. After the fourth, begin the long aftermath without an immediate reward. The final user-directed payoff comes later: a few tears and her look towards him. No returned smile, hug, verbal agreement or cure.

The original C28 broad closed-eye smile is not my first choice for this new treatment. It was accepted in a prior context with fantasy planes; without that context it can look like contentment and aestheticise the decision. Use a flatter unavailable expression, or commission one restrained new portrait rather than borrowing a broad smile because the file is available. The emotional counterpoint to her summer laugh is **absence of spontaneous reciprocity**, not a second, prettier sort of happiness.

## Updated final shot — O04, tears without an answer

This follows the parent's latest user steering after the first draft. Use the final190frames, approximately269.875–277.792s, as one sustained movie shot rather than an explanatory montage. Start with her supported, still-depleted face and him beside/off-axis. A lower eyelid wets; a few tears travel normally down her cheek. Her attention leaves the perfected objects and genuinely finds him. Hold the eye contact through the end, with a small unguarded breath or uncertainty in the mouth, **not a recovery smile**. Do not return to the old lowered-gaze tail.

The paradox is that crying can look more alive than the composed opening, while the tears might equally be grief. The image does not translate them for us. His response can be a restrained breath and changed attention, not victorious relief or taking her body into a rescue tableau. Neither character explains what she has decided now. Keep ordinary light and real two-person spatial connection; no heavenly light, deathward glance, symbolic disappearance or final intercut to band celebration. Few tears, connected anatomy and coherent eye lines matter more than an exaggerated crying effect.

## Band direction

The band is a second expressive register, not literal rescuers entering the kitchen. Yaro is the same character/voice across performance and story; jacket versus overshirt distinguishes domains conventionally. The keyboardist, guitarist, bassist and drummer stay musicians. Do not turn them into a chorus of clinicians or a literal audience judging her.

Keep the consolidated stage plot: keys rear audience-left, guitar front-left, Yaro front-centre, bass front-right, drums rear-centre on riser. A re-entry wide restores geography. Preserve the keyboardist's sole cross and spiky-haired/full-beard identity; neutral jewellery on everyone else. The blonde guitarist is not the narrative redhead. Feature each player according to audible energy, with physical support, realistic handedness and no extra sticks/fingers or changing instrument hardware. Instrument movement must be reviewed against the source music; static pose-sheet approval does not establish note or pedal accuracy.

Camera grammar: the room is contained, observant and symmetrical; the summer is close, warm and a little unguarded; the band can travel in restrained lateral arcs and modest changes of height. At the bridge, even the band camera settles. The first plea breaks stillness, rather than simply adding more gold effects. Avoid gratuitous shake, warp zooms, fake slow motion or low-angle heroic singer imagery that changes caring fear into domination.

## Primary reference principles

These are formal inspirations, not claims of shot-count analysis or instructions to copy plots:

- [Linkin Park — Numb, official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kXYiU_JCYtU), grounded also by [Warner's artist video catalogue](https://wmg.jp/linkin_park/mv/page3): a recognisable protagonist domain can alternate with a separately established performance world without requiring every line to become a literal event. Transfer the clarity of domain and emotional recurrence, not the protagonist's plot.
- [Evanescence — Everybody's Fool, official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jhC1pI76Rqo), linked through [the artist's media gallery](https://www.evanescence.com/media-gallery): performance/narrative contrast can expose a difference between outward surface and inner reality. Here use composed order versus emotional absence; do not borrow the commercial persona story or its imagery.
- [Amy Lee's first-person discussion of developing the treatment with Philipp Stölzl](https://www.nme.com/features/music-interviews/amy-lee-evanescence-the-bitter-truth-2902372) supports beginning with an emotional argument and collaborating on its film language rather than treating a band video as arbitrary performance coverage. No claim about a different director is made.
- The research agent's [Samaritans drama/film guidance](https://www.samaritans.org/documents/1039/Guidance_on_depictions_of_suicide_and_self-harm_in_drama_and_film_Digital.pdf) informs the distinction between concerning apparent calm before a crisis and presenting death as peace/reward. The film belongs to continuing life and uncertain connection.

## Outside-judge pass criteria

A viewer seeing the new cut without the treatment should be able to distinguish present, summer memory and stage immediately; identify that his early fear concerns her inaccessible composure; understand the bridge as recognition of her private decision; and experience all four pleas as desperate caring attempts, not an angry performance victory. The same viewer should not need future-season imagery, a clinic plot or an explanatory caption to follow it. Independent review must check both complete-song pacing and consecutive-frame mechanics. A generation completing or a single good still does not clear either requirement.
