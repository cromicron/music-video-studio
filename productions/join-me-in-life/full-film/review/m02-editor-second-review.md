# Original M02 — independent animation-editor review

Reviewed 2026-11-07. **Recommendation: preserve the performance and repair her face/identity before reusing the complete five-second shot.** The original has a useful, continuous playful exchange; it does not need replacement acting merely because mouths open broadly or hands overlap. However, the later female face departs materially from A01/A02 and the starting summer drawing. I would not clear the whole original as-is for the new full-film cut. Publication and previous inclusion in R4 do not decide this review.

**Why the face issue is material:** [A01/A02 comparison and source frames](m02-editor-second-review/identity-comparison.jpg) distinguish her established green/hazel eyes, defined adult facial structure and freckled drawn treatment from the later rounded blue-eyed face. Her auburn hair, freckles in places and outfit still make her recognizable, but recognition alone does not maintain the selected character design. The mismatch remains during quiet closed-mouth poses and at f121; it cannot be explained solely by laughter, eyelid opening, head angle or brief blur. It is visible in the [960-wide playback capture at 2.3 s](m02-editor-second-review/playback-source-2.30.jpg), and the R4 female portrait makes it more prominent. This is a sustained face treatment issue over roughly the last 4.5 seconds, not a two-frame cleanup. A simple iris recolor alone would leave the proportion/style change.

**What should survive a repair:** his phrase → her attention/response → reciprocal amusement → relaxed aftermath; ordinary mouth closures and small head/body bobs; white shared earphones; the original 24 fps timing. Summer may be openly joyful and she may join the playful mouthing. A01/A02 supply identity, not a requirement to copy their neutral or crisis expressions. Full-song phoneme sync is not established by this silent clip.

[Hand/contact inspection](m02-editor-second-review/hand-contact-detail.jpg) found no material extra-limb, detached wrist, floating seated support or contact failure. His hand stays at the earbud; his other hand rests by the knee. Her clasp changes around f51–61, but the wrists and two-hand overlap remain understandable. Occluded fingers need not all be exposed. His wristband's stud pattern gains detail/rows and some small accessory lines drift; these are minor continuity defects, not the reason for rejecting the complete shot. The bare forearms, washed-black tees, moth graphic, fitted charcoal trousers and restrained belt/chains retain the intended summer wardrobe. The courtyard remains drawn and spatially stable.
