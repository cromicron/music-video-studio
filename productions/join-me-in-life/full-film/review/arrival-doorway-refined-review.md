## His arrival

**Identity, style and emotion:** Brown tousled hair, green eyes, light beard/moustache, black open overshirt/tee and round pendant remain compatible with A01 and the original arrival. The redraw gives larger, more legible eyes and stronger face/neck shading; that is a close-up interpretation, not identical facial geometry. Raised inner brows, tense parted lips and the upward/sideward look read as apprehension, without a smile or confident rescue pose. Detailed linework remains drawn anime; glass and snow are softened but do not introduce a photographic character.

**Anatomy:** Jaw, ear, neck and collar connect coherently. The neck is long and strongly shaded, as in the original, but does not show a visibly detached head or twisted collarbone. The far shoulder/arm is occluded by the door upright; this does not prove the unseen arm path. No new conspicuous shoulder discontinuity was found.

**Hand and door:** The visible grip reads as four curled fingers over the near metal pull bar, with separate nails/knuckles and attached rings. The thumb is hidden around/behind the grip, not demonstrably absent. Finger-to-bar contact is readable, without an obvious floating palm or bar passing through a visible finger. The adjacent paired handle, glass uprights and snowy outside view retain the original layout. Lower handle attachments are outside the source crop, so the complete mechanism cannot be certified. A small dark studded crescent beneath the fingers remains ambiguous as wrist/accessory detail; the wrist itself is partly occluded. It is not a clear extra digit, but **do not extrapolate this small detail into a wrist/bracelet close-up or assume a generated gripping/pulling action will be correct**.

**Static verdict:** Eligible as an apprehensive arrival portrait. Not an independently validated door-opening or hand-action keyframe sequence. Preserve visible finger/bar contact; do not zoom into the ambiguous underside of the grip.

## Her doorway

Freckles and eyelid/lip detail are more pronounced in the redraw. Her gaze is lowered to screen-right, with heavy lids and a restrained mouth; she reads as withdrawn and depleted, not reassuring or suddenly recovered. The profile is recognizably the same character rather than an unrelated replacement face.

**Anatomy and setting:** The jaw-to-neck line, neck into the collar and relaxed shoulder slope remain continuous. Her neck is stylized and slender, but no broken alignment or detached shoulder is visible. Hands lie outside this portrait; no hand anatomy is certified. The left doorway/frame and rectangular fitting, far door, picture, cabinet, plant and ceiling retain the original room arrangement. Background blur does not hide a conspicuous warped window, wall break or new room transition. Hardware is environmental detail, not proof of a working lock/door mechanism.

**Static verdict:** Eligible as an inward, unsettled portrait. Existing crown headroom is very small and the current tight camera clips it; address the crop below before automatically carrying the old camera over.

## Snowy journey

**Identity and continuity:** The rear view retains his brown hair, black overshirt, rolled sleeves and black trousers/shoes. Facial identity cannot be evaluated from this angle. Hands are plausibly hidden in the pockets; the visible forearms join the sleeves and pocket area without extra exposed digits. The snowy night path, footprints, bare trees, warm lamps and lit entrance remain the same journey setting. Trees, building/window spacing, entrance detail, footprint shapes and his scale/placement are redrawn. The footprints should not be treated as a timed record of a complete walking cycle, nor is the distant doorway a geometric match to every subsequent hospital angle.

**Static gait/anatomy:** Two legs remain separately traceable from the hips. The figure's left leg (screen-left) bends back and exposes the rear sole; the opposite, straighter leg reads as the support leg, with the torso broadly over it. This is a plausible moment around toe-off, not a visibly impossible backward knee or an extra leg. The sole's heel is raised and its lower/toe area is close to the snow. **The planted shoe is largely overlapped by the rear shoe and trouser hem**: a narrow side/contact area is visible, but there is insufficient separation to certify a fully resolved planted sole or clearance throughout a step. I found no independently obvious floating-body defect in the wide still; this occlusion remains a meaningful limitation for future gait generation.

**Static verdict:** Eligible as a wide illustrated journey pose. The present still and a camera move over it do not prove walking. Any generated continuation must separately show stable ground contact, alternating support, consistent limb length and no foot sliding; do not approve it from this source alone.
