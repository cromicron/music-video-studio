# Independent review of recovered Runway footage

6 November 2026. These are three previously generated clips recovered from the connected account. This review spent **zero new generation credits**. Recovery does not make earlier paid footage free to reproduce for later songs.

**No clip or subrange is approved for the current detailed-anime cut.** The characters and wardrobe remain recognizable in parts, but all three carry a strong charcoal/paper-grain surface treatment that differs from the cleaner approved A01/A02/current-film art. The first and third clips preserve identity better than the side-hug attempt, yet their central action stalls in an awkward held hand gesture. These may remain alternate-treatment development evidence; they should not silently enter the current edit as accepted final animation.

## Review coverage

For each clip, I checked metadata/hash and inspected **20 chronological samples spanning the entire duration**, including first and last frames. I then inspected eight selected native 1280 × 720 frames across the three clips for hands, face treatment and head support. This is a frame-based review, **not normal-speed playback**. I have not inspected every intermediate frame, and no full-motion pass is claimed.

## ce69cac7: restrained acting with hovering hand

- **0.000–about 0.8 s:** seated identities and lowered gaze are relatively stable. He looks toward her rather than at the viewer. This prefix supplies very little actual action and retains the alternate paper texture; it is not a cleared animation replacement.
- **About 1.04–1.58 s:** he raises the hand nearest her from his lap, spreading the fingers beside her cheek/shoulder. At [1.583 s](recovered-ce69cac7-1.583s.png), it appears in front of her hair/collar rather than resting convincingly on her shoulder. The moving hand is softer than the held artwork.
- **About 1.58–5.00 s:** the hand remains splayed beside her face for more than three seconds, with little change and no clear completed contact or withdrawal. The held pose at [3.417 s](recovered-ce69cac7-3.417s.png) looks like an unfinished gesture, not a natural sustained side embrace. Do not describe this as a completed hug.
- Her head remains self-supported and her gaze withdrawn. No obvious gross extra digit or detached limb appears in the inspected native frames. The failure is the action's awkward endpoint and style integration, not an invented anatomical catastrophe.

**Usable range for current cut: none approved.** A quiet prefix may be retained as a research candidate, but claiming it as a complete natural action would overstate what exists.

## fd860a37: side-hug attempt

- **0.000–0.208 s:** initial seated pose resembles the other two clips; this brief opening does not establish useful action.
- **About 0.417–0.833 s:** eyes and mouths expand into a much broader cartoon treatment. At [0.625 s](recovered-fd860a37-0.625s.png), his raised posture, very broad white eye shapes and open mouth visibly depart from A01/A02's detailed restrained faces; her eyes also become enlarged and strongly outlined.
- **About 1.042–1.667 s:** he moves across into her space with two arms, one behind her and one across her front. At [1.458 s](recovered-fd860a37-1.458s.png), the body lean and open-mouth performance dominate the shot. The head approaches/rests against her hair rather than clearly retaining the earlier self-supported, frightened-but-present posture.
- **About 1.667–3.583 s:** his mouth repeatedly opens in large speech/cry shapes while he leans against her. The [2.542 s frame](recovered-fd860a37-2.542s.png) makes his distress the main performed action; she has closed eyes and becomes the still support surface. This risks the exact emotional reversal the production notes reject. It is not a silent, restrained side-hug performance.
- **At 4.000 s:** the [final pose](recovered-fd860a37-4.000s.png) is quieter, but the two-arm hold and leaned head remain. A single acceptable-looking endpoint cannot validate the preceding motion, and it should not be used to imply a repaired continuous action.

**Usable range for current cut: none approved.** Reject for facial/style drift, exaggerated mouth action and incompatible head/support/emotional staging. The static frame evidence is sufficient to reject these specific choices without making a blanket claim about all Runway generation.

**Usable range for current cut: none approved.** This remains a reference for a quieter emotional treatment, not accepted final motion.

## Reuse decision

Keep all files, hashes and rejection evidence. Do not spend credits to re-download or re-generate them. Do not loop, reverse or lengthen the held-hand portions to manufacture coverage. A deliberate alternate paper-finish version would still need clean action, consistent treatment across shots and its own normal-speed review; the different surface texture alone is not proof of better or worse art, but it prevents silent interchange with the current approved treatment.
