- **Faces are the principal blocker.** See the [input/first/intermediate/final native comparison](ltx13b-summer-independent/input-to-final-native-faces.jpg). His moustache largely drops out while jaw shadow/soul-patch shading remains; this is not a claim that all facial hair disappears. Both eyes, noses, cheeks and smiles become broader/simpler. Her enlarged dark eye shapes and lost freckles are particularly clear in the final native frame. Camera enlargement does not explain the change in drawing and features.
- **Early hand detail needs repair.** [Native hand sheets](ltx13b-summer-independent/hands-native-02.jpg) show the trails. These are blurred/ghosted contours, not proof that a second anatomical hand exists. Some blur is natural in a quick movement; the persistent missing ring after it settles is a separate continuity defect. The later visible hand has a readable curled-finger arrangement and continuous wrist; no gross hand-count failure is asserted there. [Resting-hand comparison](ltx13b-summer-independent/jewelry-resting-hands-native.jpg) prevents incorrectly claiming all his rings vanish: the left hand's visible bands mostly survive.
- **Earbud wires mostly retain their connections.** Their Y-junction already lies against his upper arm in the supplied image; do not blame the generator for inventing that placement. During the hand crossing, contours/wire contact soften around f22–26. Afterward the ear-to-junction paths remain readable. Earbud shapes enlarge/simplify with the changed faces, but no sustained floating disconnected wire was established.
- **Warmth and body support work at the broad level.** They remain seated in the same drawn courtyard, in the correct light black summer tops and fitted dark jeans. The response is staggered rather than merely simultaneous generic swaying. No face/head detachment, major torso slide, extra character, hard reset or photographic-background change was found in the inspected sequence.
- **The camera is not locked.** It pushes from the knees-in composition toward a closer two-shot, increasingly cropping lap hands. This is a prompt deviation and would affect joins, although a smooth push-in is not inherently unnatural. The intended original first-image detail is also already softened in f0.
