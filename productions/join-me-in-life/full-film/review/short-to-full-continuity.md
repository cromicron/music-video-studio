1. **Earn the first 100 s around the known middle.** Show unsettling calm/absence, his urgent arrival, then shared waiting. New room/snow/arrival art extends the Short sensibly; a sister/new cast member or a literal shot for every lyric is unnecessary. The hospital exterior, room and corridor should not delay all emotional evidence. Her doorway pose looks more alert than the established crisis close-up; keep its motion/expression depleted rather than confident.
2. **Restore the two defining actions before mass production.** Hospital must show contact→pull→held aftermath once and continuously, with no role reversal/reset. Summer must show a clear response between the same people, then natural settling. A successful window blink is a secondary technical test, not proof these central scenes work. The next production gates are hospital, summer and the screamed bridge, checked with the actual song at normal speed plus sequential frames.
3. **Maintain identity, cast and wardrobes at usable resolution.** The new panels mostly preserve the two-person cast and present-day clothes; however seven are only about 546–551×306. The current 960×540 full export enlarges them, below the source detail of R4's 1916×1080 action and large plates. This is acceptable for planning, not a silent quality downgrade in the finished film. His arrival face/beard and her new profile need A01/A02 comparison in motion; no generic replacement faces, photographic stage drift or reverted summer outfit. Refined window 1672×941 is a separate candidate, not yet the active source or a cure for the other small panels.
4. **Give later repetitions a consequence.** The plan uses 18 still sources for 58 segments; `her_window` alone appears 8 times / 37.583 s. Repetition can convey waiting, but after the central contact it must carry its changed physical state and emotional pressure. The synth solo and return to summer should deepen that contrast, not merely cycle blue rooms. Every shot need not contain body motion; the crucial actions must actually happen.
5. **All four bridge pleas must register.** S044–47 map approximately 207.875–223.500 s; the first is already a scream. Two copies of one pleading still plus unchanged reactions do not establish escalation. Preserve vocal/crowd force, a coherent reaching gesture and her unresolved reality without turning the plea into a command. Full-song timing outside the known Short remains approximate until listening review.
6. **Dawn is continuation, not cure.** Current indoor→empty courtyard→outdoor order is coherent, but the final 54 s of dawn/room motifs must earn continued presence instead of manufacturing a romantic happy ending. Keep both exhausted/affected; no automatic smile, reassuring recovery or unexplained return to a pre-contact pose. Made-bed/untidy-room motifs are optional symbolic continuity, not evidence somebody literally returned home offscreen. The proposed ending remains unapproved creative direction.
7. **Retain delivery knowledge.** Original wordmark and original song; deliberate wide/portrait framing without stretched faces; no cropped hands/shoes; correct whole-song tail through 277.776 s. Reuse the 14 reviewed Short lyric cues if captions are chosen, not raw ASR wording. A higher-resolution encode, four-minute duration, technical playback or absence of gross warps does not itself meet the acting standard.
