## What changes through the film

He begins by trying to show her ordinary futures. Their beauty cannot reach her. The remembered summer then proves what is missing: she was a bundle of joy, not a wistful ornamental smile. Returning to the same face in the present breaks the illusion that he can simply talk her back. The possible rooms become a trap until he recognizes that her calm is a rehearsed ending. All four pleas are the exposed heart of the film. Afterwards he stops arranging a perfect answer and stays in an imperfect room. Her one unscripted movement of her own cup is the smallest possible breach in certainty, not a cure.
