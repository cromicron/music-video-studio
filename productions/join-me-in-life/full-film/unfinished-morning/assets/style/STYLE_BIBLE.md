# Unfinished Morning visual production bible

The selected C eight-panel image is the composition and emotional-color reference. A01/A02 remain face/body identity authorities. This bible applies JML-036/037 to new art; it does not import the rejected clinic story.

## Identity and acting

Both are adults in the established detailed drawn anime treatment. Jaro: tousled brown hair, blue-grey eyes, narrow stubble/light beard, angular but human face, pendant, modest rings and black wrist strap. Woman: long auburn hair, green-hazel eyes, freckles, the familiar narrow oval face; do not replace with a generic round-faced blue-eyed anime girl. Her summer face must still be her present face.

Present: his black tee and open casual black overshirt, worn dark jeans, necklace/strap. Her black tee over purple-black striped sleeves and blue jeans. Outside winter, if later introduced, both wear believable warm alternative-rock layers; no formal bourgeois coat and no underdressed snow shots.

Summer: lighter washed-black short-sleeved cotton tees, bare forearms, fitted charcoal jeans. Her purple moth graphic, pendants, belt/chain remain restrained. His jacket/overshirt absent. Private shared white wired earbuds may connect the joyful singing to an ordinary playback source. No microphone, audience, band role, concert lighting or professional duet.

Her memory acting is exuberant and unguarded: confident wrong lyric, sideways shared joke, shoulders relaxing into whole-face laughter. It is not merely a soft romantic smile. Present withdrawal remains physically adjacent but emotionally unreachable, with frightened searching attention from him. The four pleas are full-force caring fear, not rage aimed at her. No instant cure.

## Worlds

- Solid present: blue/violet winter night in their modest kitchen. Drawn window, snowy bare branches outside, simple cabinets and sink, small dark wooden table, two ordinary cream mugs. No medical fixtures.
- Real summer memory: coherent physical courtyard/garden or sunlit domestic space, amber skin light and living green foliage, easy spontaneous contact. Distinguish it from imagined futures through solidity and casual lived detail.
- Imagined futures: translucent warm gold architectural possibilities, ordinary shared life. Impossible spatial folds belong to room/light/time, not malformed bodies or merged faces. Single-shot sources must not bake in a collage of extra faces unless specifically selected as an intentionally designed effect.

## Present room continuity

From the C front two-shot, Jaro sits screen-left, woman screen-right on separate adjacent chairs at the same side of the table. Window behind them toward left, kitchen counter/cabinets and sink toward right. The overhead view is a consistent reverse orientation, not a different kitchen. Keep one woman, one Jaro, two mugs and stable chair count. Original C is concept art, so exact future shot layouts must bind to one established master before generation.

## Production frames

Each generated source is one cinematic moment in 16:9, edge-to-edge artwork, no storyboard borders, labels, text, or multiple simultaneous gesture phases. Preserve complete relevant hand/arm anatomy and enough breathing room for animation; crop consciously where hands are unnecessary. Keep linework and graphic environments consistent rather than mixing photographed backgrounds with drawn faces. Source approval and movement approval remain distinct.
