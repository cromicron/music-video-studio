# C — Unfinished Morning

Independent image-only director proposal. One generated eight-panel sheet, four panels per row; no animation, render or GPU job. Source: the complete lyrics and approved A01/A02 character/style references; no previous storyboard was consulted.

**Premise:** A whole possible life folds into one kitchen at night. He imagines the ordinary mornings they could still share. She sits beside him, composed and emotionally unreachable, having already privately decided against continuing. His fear grows as every possibility collapses into her settled present. The emotional register is Love Metal: intimate grief, aching beauty, warmth and stubborn hope.

1. **Intro / verse 1:** the impeccably tidied kitchen, washed cups, physically adjacent chairs. His searching face meets her closed stillness.
2. **Pre-chorus / first chorus:** pale-gold possible mornings unfold around their solid blue present. These are his wishes, not events that have happened.
3. **Verse 2, wrong summer lyrics:** a real memory interrupts the unreal futures. Shared ridiculous singing and laughter supply the connection worth pleading for.
4. **Second pre-chorus / chorus:** ordinary futures and summer reflections circle the table but cannot reach her. His promises remain imagined; no clinic arrival or sister-call rescue occurs.
5. **Synth solo:** seasons become a luminous spiral of their modest room, folding into the same night. Scale and beauty become emotionally claustrophobic.
6. **Bridge / rehearsed final part:** her almost courteous farewell calm fills the screen. All futures compress to one thin line; his frightened recognition is visible behind her. This is the psychological trigger.
7. **All four Forget your lines:** four clearly screaming temporal exposures of him, full force from the first. Open hands, tears, raised fearful brows: desperate caring appeal, never aggression toward her. Golden possibilities swell again around the still-present pair. Each exposure represents one plea; an eventual film must give all four their full musical duration.
8. **Final chorus / outro:** two living people, cups left out, uncertain company. He remains exhausted and she remains withdrawn. A little ordinary window light and an unfinished tabletop reflection allow possibility without promising a cure.

**Visual rules:** fine detailed drawn anime; blue/violet present, amber/green remembered summer, translucent gold imagined futures. Keep environments illustrated. Present action stays indoors: his black tee/open casual black overshirt and her black top/purple-black striped sleeves preserve the reference identity. Any later exterior winter expansion needs believable warm alternative-rock layers, never a formal bourgeois coat.

**Inspection:** all eight panels present and numbered; character identity and casual wardrobe retained; summer warmth and four full-force climax exposures legible; no method, attempt, death, literal rescue, or instant recovery depicted.
