### How it actually works

1. ChatGPT generated a neutral transparent character, a closed-eye version, a lowered-gaze version, a three-quarter portrait, and a separate dusk-lake background.
2. Blender maps these images to planes. The character plane is subdivided into a 24 × 36 grid for deformation.
3. A native four-bone Blender armature provides slight breathing, head tilt, and left/right hair movement through vertex weights and keyframes.
4. Material nodes blend the eye drawings within a localized soft eye mask. This is a limited expression technique, not a fully rigged face.
5. A separate procedural mist plane and an orthographic camera provide atmosphere and gentle camera movement.
6. The alternate three-quarter drawing replaces the first pose at frame 97. **This is an editorial cut, not a continuous head turn.**
7. Cycles renders PNG frames. FFmpeg encodes the finished MP4.
8. All five source images are packed inside the `.blend`; the ZIP also contains external PNG copies and the build script.

The script automates Blender's own features. It does not implement a separate animation engine. The character is largely one deforming illustrated plane, not a professionally separated puppet with independent eyes, mouth, arms, torso, and hair layers.

## 5. Limits and lessons already learned

| Finding | Consequence for continuation |
| --- | --- |
| Independently generated head poses did not align closely enough. | Large interpolated turns produced doubled nose, jaw, and hair outlines. Do not repeat that approach blindly. |
| Additional generated in-between angles were unreliable. | Use deliberate replacement drawings, cuts, or a properly prepared rig for larger changes of angle. |
| One expression caused a transparency problem. | Alpha edges and eye-transition frames need inspection; the delivered Blender example was corrected. |
| Small aligned eye changes work more readily than large face morphs. | Start from constrained actions and verify that emotional changes are actually legible. |
| A flat transparent PNG is still flattened artwork. | It is not a layered PSD, a complete puppet, or a finished reusable rig. Hidden areas and separate parts still need preparation. |
| The pilot only demonstrates limited portrait animation. | Walking, interactions, convincing singing, continuous turns, and major gestures remain substantial work. |
| A four-minute edit is a goal, not a completed capability benchmark. | Validate a multi-shot short before promising production quality at full length. |
