# Independent static-art review — band pack v1

Reviewer: separate agent from the image creator. This is visual inspection of the saved PNGs at their available resolution, using the production criteria in [research.md](research.md). It is not user approval, animated-motion approval, verified instrumental fingering, or a claim of exact song synchronization.

## Current review boundary

The four new character turnarounds/expression sheets and four instrumental pose sheets pass their scoped static-reference checks. Existing Yaro identity remains governed by the retained original angle/expression/performance sources rather than by small facial details in the new group art. The supplied mixed lineup supersedes the earlier all-male proposal; the red-haired narrative woman remains outside the band.

The original extra drumstick and three-machine/four-string bass defects were real findings. Corrected instrumental keeper and intermediate lineup repairs are separately reviewed below; historical failed images stay excluded from generation authority. Final lineup and wide-stage pendant repairs now pass: the guitarist's ornament is unmistakably circular rather than four-armed; only the keyboardist has the visible cross. The current eleven new PNGs pass their scoped static-reference purposes, with the limits below. No unresolved blocking static identity/prop-count/pendant-clarity finding remains for this pack.

The stage plot's JSON coordinates, editable SVG and rendered PNG describe a **planning arrangement**, not a measured venue or calibrated projection. Its labels, front/rear direction and five-role placement have been checked; source JSON/XML parse and PNG decode succeeded. The perspective stage art preserves the broad left/right/back/front relationships. These checks do not certify footprints, collisions, live PA setup, foot technique or instrumental synchronization.

No new generated reference sheet is a separated puppet rig or a validated motion clip. Before using a chosen panel as a music-video source, crop/select its intended view, preserve the authoritative identity/instrument references, verify shot-specific hand contacts and supports, and inspect actual generated motion consecutively and at normal speed against the song. Exact lip sync, note choice and beat-accurate fingering remain outside this static pack.

## Rules carried forward

- Dario/keyboardist alone wears the cross. Other band jewelry is neutral. The red-haired narrative woman does not become the blonde guitarist.
- The keyboardist's newer supplied anime face outranks the earlier generated silver-streak concept. Outfit follows the supplied leather-jacket/graphic-tee/cross reference; plain black pose templates are not an independent costume lock.
- Use source-specific neutral turnarounds for identity; use reviewed instrument/pose views for contacts and supports. Do not treat the lineup's tiny finger details as a musical instruction sheet.
- Any single pose used for animation still needs a source-plate check, actual performance generation, consecutive-frame motion/contact review, and playback against the song.
